What your procurement team will ask, answered upfront

Getting a new supplier approved usually takes longer than the print job itself. So here is what most vendor forms ask for, in one place. The business is Printing Italy di Angelo Cipolla, Via Lorenzo Bonincontri 26, 00147 Roma, VAT number IT02452700590. We have been in Rome since 2008 and in print since 1987.

Every order comes with a proper Italian electronic invoice (fattura elettronica), sent through the national SDI system for Italian companies, and as a PDF for companies abroad. Each delivery has a transport document (DDT) listing what was shipped, which most finance departments need to close the purchase order.

Quotes and purchase orders

If your company needs a formal quote before raising a PO, we send one as a PDF with a reference number, the exact specifications and the validity date. The same reference appears on the invoice and on the delivery note, so matching the three documents is easy.

For regular clients we can also prepare a personal ordering page with your products, prices and deadlines already filled in. Your colleagues in different offices order from the same page and get the same price and the same quality, without anyone having to request a new quote every time. Payment can be by card through Stripe at the moment of ordering, or, for regular clients, on the monthly invoice described above.

Questions we often get
