Multi-address delivery

One print run, several addresses.

Split an order across offices, shops, hotels or event venues. Each address gets its own labelled boxes, and you receive a tracking number for every shipment.

Send a list with each address, contact name and phone, and the quantity per address.

Venues and hotels

Tell us where it has to go.

For a stand, send the venue, hall and stand number. For a hotel, the guest name and check-in date, so reception knows to hold the parcel.

Packing

How large runs are packed.

Boxes

Most orders travel in sealed cardboard boxes, each labelled with the contents and quantity. Boxes are kept to a weight one person can carry, so they can go straight to a stand or an office floor.

Pallets

Bulky runs of brochures, magazines or catalogues are stacked on pallets and shrink-wrapped. Pallet delivery needs a loading bay or a forklift at the address; tell us if there isn't one and we ship in boxes instead.
