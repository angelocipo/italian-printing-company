Not a copy shop. A printing company around the corner.

If you searched for a print shop near you, you probably have something specific in mind: posters for an event, a banner for your stand, cards before a meeting. That is exactly what we do every day. We have been printing since 1987 and in Rome since 2008, and we run our own production, so we decide the timing, not a supplier somewhere else.

Most of our customers are businesses, conference organisers and exhibitors at Fiera di Roma, La Nuvola and the hotels in EUR, plus a fair number of couples getting married in Italy. Many of them are not Italian, which is why we work in English as easily as in Italian. You send the file, we check it, send you a proof and print.

Urgent printing

Need it today, or by tomorrow morning?

We are not a 24-hour shop and we are closed on Sunday. What we do have is our own machines and short production times: if your file is ready in the morning, many jobs can be picked up or delivered in Rome the same day. For really tight deadlines we have Night Shift, where we print overnight and deliver the next morning.

The quickest way to know if it can be done is to send a WhatsApp message with what you need and when. We tell you straight away, honestly, whether it works.

Prices and quotes

What does it cost?

Printing prices depend on four things: size, material, quantity and deadline. That is why most print shops ask for details before they give a number, and so do we. The difference is that you get a written quote quickly, usually within two hours during opening hours, with VAT and delivery included so there are no surprises later.

Tell us what you need, how many, and by when. If you are not sure about the material or the size, say so: we suggest what works best for where it will hang or be handed out.

Questions people ask us

Where is your print shop in Rome?

At Via Lorenzo Bonincontri 26, 00147 Roma, in the EUR area, a short drive from Fiera di Roma and La Nuvola. You can pick up your order or have it delivered to your hotel, office or stand.

Are you open on Sunday or 24 hours?

No. We are open Monday to Friday 8:00–12:00 and 15:00–18:00, and Saturday 8:00–12:00. For urgent jobs, message us on WhatsApp: with Night Shift production we can often print overnight and deliver the next morning.

Can you print A1 posters, foam board or PVC banners?

Yes. Posters from A3 to A0 and custom sizes, foam board and PVC panels (forex, also called sunboard), PVC and mesh banners, roll-ups and signs for shops, stands and events.

How much does printing cost?

It depends on size, material, quantity and deadline. A roll-up banner 80/85 × 200 cm is € 70 including VAT. For everything else, send us the details and we reply with a written quote, usually within two hours during opening hours.

Do you print t-shirts and embroidered clothing?

Yes. We print and embroider t-shirts, polos and staff uniforms with your logo, from a single piece to larger runs, with sizes and colours mixed in one order.

Can I just print a few documents?

We are a production print shop, so we focus on printed products: posters, roll-ups, business cards, flyers, booklets and clothing. For a few pages of documents, a copy shop near your hotel will usually be quicker.

Tell us what you need to print.

We reply Mon–Fri 8–12 / 15–18 and Saturday 8–12 (CET), usually within two hours.
