Honest pricing

What printing honestly costs in Italy.

Foreigners fear being overcharged in a country where they don't speak the language. The fear points the wrong way. Standard-timing printing in Italy is generally cheaper than in the Netherlands, and Northern European prices climb by the day. The real risk in Italy isn't price. It's timing — which is exactly what we solve.

Our pricing philosophy is Dutch: a quote is a quote. If we make a mistake in the quote, we absorb it. The only exception is when the mistake is in your favor. A client once paid for A4 prints when the job was actually A5. We refunded the difference before they noticed anything was wrong.

Rush work costs more, and we say so openly. Urgent printing with 1-hour courier delivery costs real money to operate, and honest shops charge for it instead of pretending urgency is free.
