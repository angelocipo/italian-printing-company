Not every printing job in Rome is a trade-show banner

Angelo Cipolla, Rome

Most weeks the shop is printing roll-up banners for someone's fair stand. But a fair share of visitors walk in — or message on WhatsApp — with something much smaller: a boarding pass that won't load on their phone, a hotel reservation printed as proof for a visa desk, or photos from the trip they want on paper before flying home.

The problem is usually the same

Hotel business centres are often slow, expensive, or simply don't exist anymore. And a tourist who doesn't speak Italian can burn an hour trying to explain what should be a two-minute job at a print counter that doesn't do walk-ins in English.

What actually gets asked for

Boarding passes and check-in documents top the list, followed by printed itineraries, insurance paperwork for a clinic visit, and the occasional passport photo before a same-day appointment.

Same shop, smaller job

These aren't large orders and they don't need to be treated like one. Send the file over WhatsApp, and it's usually printed and ready within the hour — no appointment, no minimum order.
