    To avoid any misunderstandings, please note that production of the quoted materials is considered authorized and confirmed only once payment has been received and processed. An invoice will be issued and sent to you after payment has been received.

    Production will commence only upon payment confirmation and will follow the agreed turnaround time. Any delay in payment will result in a corresponding delay in production and delivery.

  4. 4

    For production, I require either:

    • A high-resolution print-ready PDF file at 100% final size, including bleed where applicable; or

Direct communication helps prevent misunderstandings regarding file specifications, dimensions, bleed, resolution, colors, and other technical printing requirements.
