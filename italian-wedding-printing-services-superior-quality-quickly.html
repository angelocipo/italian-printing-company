What couples usually ask us to print

Most weddings we print for need the same handful of pieces: the invitations and save-the-dates, an order of service or ceremony booklet, menus for each table, place cards, and a big welcome sign or seating plan at the entrance. Some couples add a programme for the weekend, thank-you cards, or printed bags for the welcome gifts at the hotel.

Because everything is printed in one place, the colours, fonts and paper match across all of it. That matters more than people expect: a menu that is a slightly different white from the place card next to it shows up in every photo of the table.

For the large pieces, such as a seating plan or a “welcome to our wedding” sign, we print on rigid foam board in A1 or A0, so it stands on an easel without curling. If you prefer something you can roll up and carry, a roll-up banner works well at the entrance of a villa or a hotel garden.

Planning from abroad, step by step

Almost every couple we work with lives somewhere else, so we have made the process work entirely at a distance. You send the design, or a rough idea if you don't have one yet. We check the file, set it up for print and send you a proof by email or WhatsApp. Nothing is printed until you approve it.

If the guest list is mixed, we proof the Italian and English versions side by side, so a name or a date is never different between the two. Guest names on place cards come straight from your spreadsheet, which avoids the typing mistakes that usually creep in at the last minute.

Questions we often get

How far in advance should we order wedding stationery?

Ideally three to four weeks before the wedding for invitations, and one to two weeks for menus, place cards and signs. Short on time? Tell us the date: many pieces can be printed in a few days.

Can you design the invitations for us?

We work best from your own design or your designer's files, and we prepare them for print and check every detail. If you only have an idea, send it over and we can tell you what is possible.

Do we have to come to Rome to see the paper?

No. We send a proof by email and can describe or photograph the paper options. Many couples only see the printed pieces at the venue.

Can you deliver to a venue outside Rome?

Yes, anywhere in Italy. We ship by UPS and plan the printing so everything arrives a day or two before the wedding.
