Good to know

From your file to a folded brochure.

A4 here, Letter at home.

Italy uses A4 (210 × 297 mm). A US Letter file printed on A4 gets white edges or loses part of the layout. We check the format and adapt it before printing.

Folded panels aren't equal.

On a tri-fold, the panel that folds inside is a few millimetres narrower so the leaflet closes flat. If your file has three equal panels, we correct it.

Translated text runs longer.

Italian and German copy is often longer than English. Leave room in the layout, or send the final text and we check that nothing overflows.

How many to bring.

Visitors take fewer brochures than most exhibitors expect, and leftover boxes are heavy to ship home. Order a smaller run; a reprint is quick if you run out.
