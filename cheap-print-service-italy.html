02 · Materials

Cheap Printing Does Not Mean Cheap Materials

Saving money by choosing the wrong paper or compromising on production quality can make your business look less professional.

A brochure printed on suitable, substantial paper creates a different impression from one printed on stock that feels too thin. The same principle applies to business cards, presentation folders, catalogues, and other materials used to represent your company.

The objective is not to buy the cheapest possible product. It is to obtain the best balance of price, materials, print quality, and intended use.

We help you identify where you can save money without undermining the result.

04 · Print-ready files

Send Print-Ready Files and Avoid Unnecessary Costs

One of the most effective ways to keep printing affordable is to prepare your files correctly.

Before production, we check important technical details, including:

01ResolutionImages must have sufficient resolution for the final print size.
02BleedArtwork must extend beyond the final cutting line where required.
03DimensionsFiles must match the agreed final format.
04File readinessFonts, images, and artwork must be suitable for production.

If a file is unsuitable, we address the problem before printing rather than allowing a preventable mistake to reach production.

Correct files reduce the risk of reprints, delays, and additional costs. If artwork requires correction or adaptation, any additional work should be agreed upon before production.
