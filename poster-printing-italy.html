Good to know

Getting large format right.

Resolution at full size.

A logo that looks sharp on screen can print blurry at a metre wide. We check every file at its final size and tell you if an image needs replacing.

Stand walls have exact sizes.

Panel sizes vary between venues. Ask the organiser for the measurements, or send us the exhibitor manual and we find them for you.

Rolled, not folded.

Posters travel rolled in tubes, so they arrive without creases and one person can carry them.

Hanging at the venue.

Tell us how you'll hang them, with hooks, velcro or adhesive, and we print on a material that works with it.
